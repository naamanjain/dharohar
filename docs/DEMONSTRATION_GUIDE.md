# DHAROHAR — Demonstration Guide

## Open the live demonstration

**https://dharohar.urbantechx.in**

The deployed interface is bilingual (Hindi and English) and is labelled as a prototype using synthetic data.

## Demo account access

Dedicated public judge credentials are being provisioned separately from administrator and operational accounts.

**Do not use or publish the previously shared administrator/demo password.** The judge account must be a distinct account with a unique password, limited permissions, synthetic data only and no access to administration or real operational records.

Once a dedicated judge account has been provisioned and tested, its username and password may be inserted in this section and in the README. Until then, request access from the project administrator.

## Suggested reviewer walkthrough

1. **Sign-in:** inspect the Hindi/English toggle and prototype/synthetic-data notice.
2. **Dashboard:** review the available record counts and metrics. Note any metric explicitly reported as unavailable.
3. **Parcel registry:** open a parcel and inspect its identifiers, location and available geometry.
4. **Map interaction:** select a parcel and inspect its boundary. Do not assume the drawn geometry is an authoritative legal boundary.
5. **Document workflow:** inspect a synthetic document and its extracted fields.
6. **Validation:** review missing fields, arithmetic checks and surfaced discrepancies.
7. **Verification:** inspect the human-review workflow and available status transitions.
8. **Audit trail:** inspect the recorded action history and its integrity status.
9. **Logout:** sign out and verify that the session is ended.

The exact screens available depend on the demo account's assigned role and capabilities.

## Safety and use

- Every record in the public demo is synthetic.
- Do not enter names, identity numbers, parcel data, documents or other real personal information.
- Do not attempt password spraying, load testing or high-rate automated requests.
- Do not interpret displayed records, OCR output, polygon geometry or certificates as authoritative government records or legal evidence.
- Demo accounts may be reset or disabled during maintenance.

## Reporting a problem

When reporting an issue, provide the screen name, the action taken, the visible error and the approximate time. Do not include passwords, session tokens, citizen data or private operational information.
